A little space between work and home.

We are four students who wanted a little distance between the working day and the rest of life. Kitchen tables should have room for more than laptops.

That is why we created Velune. A welcoming place to focus, meet and get things done. When the day is over, home feels like home again.

Room for your working day

So, what do we offer?

Coming on your own? Settle into a quiet, modern workspace with an ergonomic chair, a spacious desk and a monitor ready for your laptop. A comfortable setting for a focused day.

Bringing others? We also offer rooms for small teams and larger groups. Whether you are sharing ideas, holding a meeting or planning a training day, there is space to get everyone together.
